Halcyon 2 is the biggest update since we launched, and — fittingly — most of it is about doing less.

## A timeline that keeps up

The timeline now renders a full year of work for a fifty-person team without a stutter. Drag a project and everything downstream moves with it.

## Focus hours

Tell Halcyon when you do your best work. It will block the time, decline what it can, and reschedule what it cannot — politely.

## The Friday digest

One email, every Friday, that answers three questions: what shipped, what slipped, and what needs a decision. Most people read it in under two minutes.

Halcyon 2 is rolling out to every workspace this week, on every plan, at no extra cost.
